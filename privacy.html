Privacy policy

Last updated: April 2026

ToolFest (“we”, “the site”) at toolfest.in provides free online calculators and file utilities. This page explains what happens to information when you use the site.

Browser-based processing

Most tools (PDF merge, image compress, word counter, EMI calculator, etc.) run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Your files and text are not sent to our servers for those features because no upload occurs.

Hosting & logs

The site may be hosted on GitHub Pages or similar static hosting. The host may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and requested URLs in server logs. We do not control those logs beyond the hosting provider’s policies.

Advertising & analytics (Google)

This site may load Google AdSense (ca-pub-1957013406466053) and Google Analytics 4 (G-Y6CJS2NH91) via assets/js/site-analytics.js. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads and traffic. Review Google’s Advertising Policies and use Google Ad Settings to manage ad preferences where available.

Third-party scripts

Some pages load open-source libraries from public CDNs (for example pdf-lib, PDF.js, QRCode) to provide functionality. Those providers may see your IP address when your browser fetches the script.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through the channel listed on your deployment (for example your domain’s contact page).
