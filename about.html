About ToolFest

ToolFest lives at toolfest.in. We publish free, practical tools for everyday document, image, money, and text tasks. The site is aimed especially at people in India and the United States who see the same problems again and again: loan EMI estimates before talking to a bank, GST on a quote, merging PDFs for an application, shrinking a photo under an upload limit, or formatting JSON from a broken API response.

Why browser-based tools?

When software runs in your browser, your files and pasted text often never leave your device. That matters for salary slips, legal drafts, baby photos, and unreleased writing. We still load fonts and optional scripts from CDNs; our Privacy Policy explains what that means in practice.

Editorial quality

Each major tool page includes an article section written for humans: what the tool does, what it cannot do, and common questions. Category hubs describe how to choose between merge, split, compress, and similar workflows. We avoid empty landing pages that exist only to carry advertisements.

How the site is funded

Hosting, domains, and maintenance take time and money. ToolFest may show Google AdSense or similar ads. Revenue supports keeping calculators and converters online without charging visitors or forcing registration.

Not professional advice

Finance tools illustrate math with standard formulas; they are not tax, legal, or investment advice. PDF and image tools cannot guarantee the same output as desktop suites for every complex file. Always verify results with qualified professionals and official portals when stakes are high.

Contact

For rights or privacy questions, use the contact channel you publish for toolfest.in (for example an email on your domain or host support). We read policy-related mail when capacity allows.
